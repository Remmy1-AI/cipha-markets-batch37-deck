Problem

Traders already have accounts.
Execution is still the bottleneck.

What traders live with today

  • Manual chart-watching and late entries/exits
  • Signal groups that tell you what—not how to execute on your account
  • Copy-trading and black-box bots that obscure risk and control
  • Prop challenges where inconsistent execution kills funded status

What they actually need

  • Controlled AI agents on the broker/prop account they already own
  • Non-custodial — capital stays with their broker
  • Transparent deliberation + code validation before any order
  • Choice: full Autonomy, or confirm with Do it

CiPHA Markets is not a signals app. Agents run on the trader’s own connected account.

Solution

Multi-agent council → validate → execute.

1. Deliberate

A multi-agent council debates the trade thesis on the trader’s connected account context.

2. Validate

Deterministic code checks the proposal before anything reaches the broker.

3. Execute

Autonomy auto-executes, or the trader confirms with Do it.

Non-custodial by design

AI agents on the trader’s own broker or prop account. CiPHA never holds funds.

Integrations live

cTrader · TradeLocker · Match-Trader · MetaApi (MT4/MT5) · Deriv

Competition

Not another signals feed.

Signals apps

Tell traders what to do. Execution and account risk stay on the human — inconsistent and slow.

Copy trading

Blind following. Little visibility into why a trade fires on your account.

Black-box bots

Opaque automation. Hard to trust for prop rules and retail risk control.

Manual trading

Full control, but fatigue and inconsistency — especially under prop constraints.

CiPHA wedge

AI agents on your broker/prop account — non-custodial. Multi-agent council deliberates, deterministic code validates, then Autonomy auto-executes or you confirm with Do it. Controlled agents, not a signals app.
